The run truth

Volts times milliamp-hours is the watt-hours the pack honestly holds; divided by the suction watts it is the minutes the robot actually runs. The box minutes were measured on eco over bare floors - your watts decide.

The coverage truth

Honest minutes times the pace, times the 0.7 revisit norm (labeled) for all the doubling back, is the square footage one charge honestly covers - mapped run or brochure.

The bin truth

House square feet times the debris norm (labeled: 15 mL tidy, 40 with a dog, 70 in shedding season) is the milliliters the floor sheds each day. The bin's liters name the days between empties.

What it never does

It never reads the eco minutes as your minutes, never counts the revisited corners twice, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the dust bunny under the bed keeps the honest ledger.

Robotvac Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
